WORKSHEET USE
The worksheet has 158 interactive fields, including eight checkboxes.
Download it and open it in a PDF reader that supports forms if a browser preview
will not let you type. Save your own completed copy. Calculations are manual.
Keep extended evidence and additional term records in attachments or separate
saved copies. Use appropriately scaled units in financial fields.

ADAPTING THE MATERIAL
Replace illustrative inputs with verified local evidence. Preserve the distinction
between regulatory requirements, commercial exchanges and voluntary benefits.
The guide is negotiation guidance, not a ready-to-sign contract or a legal opinion.
Company announcements and government frameworks are labeled by status. Fictional
numbers are examples and are not market rates. Update legal and utility information
before relying on it in a local proceeding.
